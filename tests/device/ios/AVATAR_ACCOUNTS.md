# Original Avatar account scopes and source groups

Feature 3/14 remains partial. These components prepare authority and acquire
original evidence; they do not establish client login, relationship consent,
peer rendering or complete cleanup. Native compilation and devices are unverified.

The optional `AvatarAccountScopes` belongs to the actual AccountProvider used by
the typed social authenticator. An Avatar/People fixture run allocates two
independent owned account child scopes without granting tokens or establishing
a relationship. Controlled session/domain inputs are bound once, separately
from measured native session/peer evidence. Each actual password grant and
nonce-bound protected profile has its own original account scope and proof.
Fixture activation advances the owned account and social input tickets.

The existing private HTTPS server exposes closed account status/credential
preparation routes to its reader/adapter roles, control/session preparation to
the reader, and original profile proofs to the actor. Neither the primary
adapter nor input credentials can substitute for the actor's independently
acquired product facts or publication credential. Ordinary product HTTP
requests supply consent; no native state or relationship setter is provided.
Fixtures without the optional social authority keep their existing registration
behavior and expose no Avatar account credentials.

Pending/connected relationships, unfinished social responses and actual account
handlers prevent premature authority or metadata retirement. After normal
relationship removal, explicit authority revocation removes grants/accounts;
actual in-flight handler drainage must still precede retirement. Only owned
child scopes are retired. Failed unexposed allocation rolls back known created
scopes, preserves foreign allocations, and retains unresolved responsibility.

`IOSAvatarSources` separately journals each actual original acquisition before
reading. It joins original State2, Renderer4, SocialXHR1, PeopleModel1,
AvatarScripts1, native globals and unprojected normal widgets under independently
acquired native/host clock brackets and genuine input-phase snapshots. Full
unmodified groups use AvatarOriginals private immutable files and bounded
manifests. Reconstruction restores native/source floors and retired script
membership. Partial reads or storage remain owned and cannot be replayed or
wrapped in new clocks. A phase-crossing frame is retained without becoming a
stable feature result.

Host verification uses real TLS/OAuth/profile/socket writes, phase/actor HTTP
journals and private original files. Native product DTOs remain explicit modeled
boundaries. The combined 63 account/social/source/publication/phase tests pass,
including independent account proofs, late socket completion, early retirement,
allocation rollback, source/candidate replacement, interrupted acquisition and
historical reconstruction. The whole Avatar action/image/peer/publication and
repeated cleanup coordinator remains required before enabling its full capability.
